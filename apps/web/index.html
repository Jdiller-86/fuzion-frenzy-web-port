Clear the cooling rods

Jump the low blue rods. Duck under the high orange rods. Each clearance earns one point. Misses push you back; seven misses eliminate you. Last player standing wins. Equal final best scores share a win when everyone falls together.

Solo runs last until elimination. Practice continues with the last survivor and is unranked. Jump is a press; duck is a hold. Release jump before jumping again.

Reconstruction parameters: action windows, acceleration, hazard order, hit recovery, ties and practice details remain provisional. Original assets are imported; camera, shading and duck pose are approximations.

Press a gamepad button to let your browser discover it. Standard mapping: A / bottom button jumps, B / right button ducks. Each device belongs to one seat. Shared keyboards may have rollover limits.

Seat rings: P1 green · P2 red · P3 blue · P4 pink. Focus loss pauses the round. Release held controls, then resume.
